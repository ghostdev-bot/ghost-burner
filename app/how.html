// how it works

What Ghost Burner does.
And where the 2% goes.

Every Solana token account holds a small refundable SOL deposit (rent, about 0.00204 SOL). Ghost Burner finds the token accounts you no longer need, closes them, and sends that rent back to your wallet.

  • Empty accounts are the easy win. They hold no tokens, so closing them only refunds rent.
  • Burning is opt-in. Junk tokens and standard NFTs are burned only if you tick them yourself.
  • Simulated before you sign. The review screen shows the exact SOL back, fee and net.
  • Your wallet is the only signer. No seed phrase, no approvals, no delegation.

Where the 2% fee goes

Without a referral link1% $GHOSTDEV treasury · 1% BOBO buy
With a referral link0.5% treasury · 0.5% referrer · 1% BOBO buy

You pay 2% of the SOL rent you reclaim either way. A referral only moves half of the treasury's share to the person who shared the link. Each part is a normal SOL transfer inside the same transaction, rounded down to the lamport, and never taken from your tokens.

Example: closing 10 empty accounts frees about 0.0204 SOL. The fee is about 0.000408 SOL: 0.000204 to the treasury and 0.000204 to the BOBO buy, or with a referral 0.000102 treasury, 0.000102 referrer, 0.000204 BOBO buy. You keep about 0.019992 SOL, minus Solana's network fee (about 0.000005 SOL per transaction).

What gets closed or burned

  • Empty token accounts. Pre-selected. Closing refunds the rent to you.
  • Tokens you choose to burn. Destroyed permanently, then the account is closed.
  • Standard NFTs you choose to burn. Their metadata and edition rent comes back too.
  • Never touched: wrapped SOL, frozen accounts, compressed or programmable NFTs, and accounts someone else controls. These show under Excluded.

Referral links

A link looks like ghostburner.surge.sh/?ref=Wallet. The first valid link you open is remembered in your browser for 30 days, and the burn screen shows who referred you. Your own wallet and the fee wallets can't be referrers. If the referrer's wallet can't receive a small payment yet (a brand-new wallet needs about 0.00065 SOL to exist), that part goes to the treasury instead, so the total stays 2%.
