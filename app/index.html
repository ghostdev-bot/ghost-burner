Good to know

What is rent?

Solana charges a refundable deposit to keep each account alive. A token account you no longer use still holds it. Closing the account refunds it to you.

What does burning do?

Burning permanently destroys tokens so the account can be closed. It cannot be undone. Only burn things you are sure are worthless to you.

What's not supported (v1)

Compressed NFTs (they have no token account), programmable NFTs (pNFTs), print editions, wrapped SOL, frozen accounts, accounts with withheld Token-2022 fees, and accounts whose close authority is someone else. These are listed under Excluded.
