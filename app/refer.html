Earn 0.5% of every reclaim.

When someone burns through your link, 0.5% of the SOL rent they reclaim goes straight to your wallet, paid on-chain inside their own burn transaction. No claiming, no dashboard balance, no waiting.

  • They pay nothing extra. The fee is 2% either way. Your 0.5% comes out of the treasury's share.
  • Paid instantly. A normal SOL transfer to you, in the same transaction that refunds their rent.
  • First link wins for 30 days. The first valid link someone opens is remembered in their browser for 30 days.

Where the 2% fee goes

Without a referral link1% $GHOSTDEV treasury · 1% BOBO buy
With your link0.5% treasury · 0.5% you · 1% BOBO buy

Example: closing 10 empty accounts reclaims about 0.0204 SOL, so you earn about 0.000102 SOL. Solana won't create a brand-new wallet with less than about 0.00065 SOL, so if your wallet has never held SOL, small payouts go to the treasury instead until it does. Keep a little SOL in it. Your own wallet and the fee wallets can't be referrers.
